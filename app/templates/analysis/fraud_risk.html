Risk Score Legend
4-5 Critical — multiple strong fraud indicators 3 High — several flags warrant investigation 2 Moderate — some anomalies detected 1 Low — single flag, monitor
Flag Definitions:
  • YoY Spike — Year-over-year spending increase >$500K or >200%
  • Outlier — Billing >2 standard deviations above peers on any HCPCS code
  • MoM Spike — Month-over-month spending more than doubled (>100% increase)
  • Shared Addr — 3+ providers billing from the same building address
  • Network — Bills through 5+ different servicing providers
Entity Designation Filter:

Suspicious (Shell Co / Provider Mill Indicators):

  • LLC / PLLC / LLP — Easy to form/dissolve limited liability entities
  • Enterprise / Holdings / Management — Corporate shell indicators
  • Solutions / Consulting — Vague business names
  • Staffing / Agency — Staffing agencies billing Medicaid directly
  • Supply / Supplies (DME) — DME/supply companies (high fraud area)
  • Wellness / Global / International — Vague/inflated scope names

Legitimate (Established Institutions):

  • Hospital / Community / Foundation / Center — Established institutions
  • Inc / Corp — Standard corporate entities
  • PC / PA (Professional) — Standard professional corporations
