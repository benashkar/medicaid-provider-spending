Parishes

Parishes within the Catholic Diocese of Youngstown

The Catholic Diocese of Youngstown stands as a vibrant spiritual hub, encompassing parishes across Mahoning, Trumbull, Ashtabula, Stark, Portage, and Columbiana counties. Through this expansive network, the Diocese fosters a rich tapestry of faith communities, dedicated to serving and nurturing the spiritual well-being of individuals across a diverse geographical expanse.

FINDING SACRAMENTAL RECORDS

Don’t see a former parish on our Finder? Some worship sites have merged under a new name or closed. Sacramental records are always kept intact in the new parish or at the Diocesan Archives. Joan Lawson, Diocesan Chancellor and Archivist, has developed a list of former parish names, current record locations, and our record keeping policies under Canon Law.

The Catholic Diocese of Youngstown was established on May 15, 1943, and was canonically erected on July 22, 1943. Located in Northeast Ohio, the diocese includes six counties; Ashtabula, Columbiana, Mahoning, Portage, Stark and Trumbull.
